I'm Tarun Verma — a graphic designer and web developer based in Noida, India. Over 4+ years working across branding, apparel design, digital creatives and web development, I've built a practice that sits at the intersection of visual design and front-end technology.

My background in graphic design shapes how I approach web development — with an instinct for hierarchy, composition and visual storytelling. The result is work that looks considered and performs well.

I'm currently deepening my web development practice while using design thinking as an advantage. Whether that's a brand identity, an apparel graphic, or a responsive website — the goal is always the same: intentional work that connects.
