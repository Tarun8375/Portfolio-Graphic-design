Digital Campaign

CategorySocial Media / Digital Design
Year2025
RoleGraphic Designer / Digital Designer
ServicesSocial Media Creatives, Digital Assets, Templates
Overview

Introduction

Social media creatives, digital campaign assets and visual templates — designed for consistent cross-platform brand presence. The project encompassed creating a suite of adaptable assets for Instagram, Facebook and digital ad placements.

Approach

Design Approach

The brief was to create a flexible system of digital creatives that maintained brand consistency while working across different content formats — feed posts, Stories, carousels and paid ads. Each asset was designed with clear visual hierarchy, strong focal points and brand-aligned typography.

Templates were delivered as editable files — allowing the marketing team to adapt content independently while keeping the visual system intact. The design-led approach to marketing assets means every piece feels considered, not produced.
