Kora Apparel

Category Brand Identity / E-commerce / Apparel
Year 2024
Role Brand Designer / Web Developer
Services Logo, Identity, WooCommerce, Web Design
Overview

Introduction

Kora Apparel is a contemporary apparel label requiring a complete brand identity, commercial video shoot direction, and an e-commerce presence. The project encompassed logo design, visual language, typography system, and a WooCommerce-powered online store — built to communicate the brand's aesthetic clearly and convert visitors into customers.

Challenge

The Challenge

Creating a visual identity for an apparel brand that needed to feel contemporary but timeless — distinctive enough to stand out without relying on trends. The e-commerce side needed to balance strong visual design with a conversion-focused shopping experience.

Approach

The Approach

Starting with typography and a restrained colour palette, the identity was built around a clean wordmark that works equally well on garments, digital surfaces and packaging. The WooCommerce store was designed and developed to extend the brand aesthetic — with a dark editorial layout, oversized product photography and a streamlined checkout.

Gallery
Outcome

The Outcome

A complete brand and e-commerce system — logo, colour palette, typography, brand guidelines and a fully functional WooCommerce store. The identity works cohesively across garments, digital platforms and packaging, giving Kora Apparel a clear and consistent visual presence.
