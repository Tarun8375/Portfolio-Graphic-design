Apparel Graphic Series

Category Graphic Design / Apparel
Year 2024–25
Role Graphic Designer
Services Illustration, Typography, Print-Ready Art
Overview

Introduction

An ongoing series of graphic designs created for apparel — exploring bold visual ideas through typography, illustration and composition. Each piece is designed for print-ready production, with a strong emphasis on how the artwork reads on garment.

Process

Design Process

Each graphic begins with a concept — visual culture reference, typographic idea or illustrative direction. The work is developed in Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, then prepared as print-ready files (vector, CMYK, appropriate resolution) for DTG, screen printing or embroidery depending on the application.

The series demonstrates range — from minimal typographic compositions to detailed illustrative work — while maintaining a consistent level of craft and intentionality across each piece.
